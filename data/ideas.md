Miracles:
Turning Water into Wine

Context: At the Wedding at Cana, Jesus turned six stone jars of water into wine after the hosts ran out (John 2:1-11).

Jeopardy Clue Idea: ** To save the hosts from embarrassment at Cana, Jesus performed his first recorded miracle by transforming this liquid into a fine vintage.

Walking on the Water

Context: Jesus walked across the stormy Sea of Galilee to meet his disciples in their boat (Matthew 14:22-33).

Jeopardy Clue Idea: ** During a storm on the Sea of Galilee, the disciples were terrified to see Jesus doing this, thinking he was a ghost.

Calming the Storm

Context: Jesus rebuked the wind and waves while crossing the Sea of Galilee, bringing instant calm (Mark 4:35-41).

Jeopardy Clue Idea: Awoken from a nap on a boat, Jesus asked "Why are you afraid?" after performing this miracle over the wind and waves.

The Miraculous Catch of Fish

Context: After a fruitless night of fishing, Jesus told Simon Peter to let down his nets one more time, resulting in a catch so massive it began to tear the nets (Luke 5:1-11).

Jeopardy Clue Idea: Though they had toiled all night with nothing to show for it, Peter obeyed Jesus' command to lower the nets again, resulting in this miracle.

The Coin in the Fish's Mouth

Context: To pay the temple tax, Jesus instructed Peter to cast a line and open the mouth of the first fish he caught to find a shekel coin (Matthew 17:24-27).

Jeopardy Clue Idea: To pay the temple tax for himself and Peter, Jesus used a four-drachma coin found in this unusual place.
